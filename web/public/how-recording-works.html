Raadiosalvesti field guide

How does the recording bit work?

Think of it as a very patient tape recorder. The web page writes down what you want. A small program on your computer reads the note, opens the radio stream with ffmpeg, and saves an MP3 for you.

The clock has padding

Schedules are not perfect. The recorder starts early and stays late so the opening and closing words are less likely to get chopped off.

The finished card shows the actual start and stop times. A recording longer than one hour gets split at clock-hour boundaries. Neighboring parts overlap by 30 seconds, so a sentence at the cut is not lost.

What the labels mean

The header and "Salvestused" use the same active-job status. The labels describe the recorder's current job, not a guess made by the browser.

Alustan

The job exists and ffmpeg is about to connect to the stream.

Salvestab

ffmpeg is receiving audio and the MP3 is growing on the recorder computer.

Lõpetan

You pressed "Lõpeta". The recorder checks stop commands every 3 seconds and tells ffmpeg to finish.

Viimistlemisel

Recording has stopped. The app is joining parts, measuring duration, copying the result to storage, and writing metadata.

Why can the page lag?

There are two different things to wait for. The status is small and quick. The playable audio snapshot is much larger, so it updates less often.

Seeing the recording

Where the pieces live

In local mode, the recorder, website, settings, and MP3 files all live on your computer. Your phone opens the computer's web page over Wi-Fi or Tailscale.

Questions people actually ask

The recorder has a few behaviors that look odd until you know why they exist.

Does the web page record the radio?

No. The page only changes settings and displays status. The always-on computer runs ffmpeg and does the recording. Closing the browser does not stop an active recording.

Who loads the programme schedule?

The recorder loads Kuku and Sky Media schedules. For ERR stations, the browser loads the schedule and saves the exact times of your selected episode in config.json. The recorder still captures the audio.

What happens when I press "Lõpeta"?

The page writes a one-time stop command. The recorder sees it within about 3 seconds, stops every part belonging to that recording, finishes the MP3, and keeps everything captured so far.

What if the stream freezes?

The recorder watches the file size. If it does not grow for 90 seconds, ffmpeg is restarted. If the recorder itself crashes, the next run tries to rescue an old live file and marks it "katkestatud".

Why is the default MP3 mono?

Speech radio usually does not need stereo. Mono uses 64 kbps and less storage. Stereo is optional and uses 128 kbps. The mono mixer leaves a little headroom to avoid clipping loud streams.

When do old recordings disappear?

Cleanup runs after recordings and roughly every 30 minutes. A show's "Hoia alles" value keeps that many recent programme days. Otherwise recordings stay for 14 days by default.

Where does the podcast feed fit?

The feed is another private view of finished files in rec/. It does not make extra recordings. Its secret token lets a podcast app fetch the XML and MP3 files without exposing the rest of the website.
